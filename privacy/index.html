Privacy

Last updated 15 October 2026.

web-dnd-vault

web-dnd-vault is a personal backup tool. Only the owner of this site uses it, to keep encrypted backup copies of project files in the owner's own Google Drive.

  • What it can access. It asks Google only for the drive.file permission, so it can see and change only the files it created itself. It cannot see anything else in your Google Drive.
  • What it stores. Files are encrypted on the owner's computer before upload. They stay in the owner's Google Drive.
  • Sharing. Nothing is sold, shared with anyone, or used for advertising or analytics.
  • Removing access. Revoke the app at myaccount.google.com/permissions, and delete its files from Google Drive.

web-dnd-vault's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
